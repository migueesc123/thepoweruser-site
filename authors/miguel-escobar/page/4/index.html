Miguel Escobar (33)

Senior Product Manager at Microsoft, working on Microsoft Fabric (Dataflow Gen2) and Power Query, foundational data platform technologies shipping across Excel and Power BI at massive global scale.

I focus on product strategy and platform evolution for data ingestion, transformation, and query infrastructure, partnering closely with engineering and leadership across multiple teams and regions.

Prior to Microsoft, I founded and led a Microsoft Gold Partner consultancy focused on data analytics, where I worked directly with enterprise customers on large-scale data platforms and transformation initiatives.

I’m a former Microsoft MVP, MCP, and MCT, and the author of books on data preparation and analytics.

Interested in global product leadership conversations where platform depth, scale, and long-term product foundations matter.
